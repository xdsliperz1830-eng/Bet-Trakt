    How units are recommended

    By confidence. 1 = 0.5u, 2 = 1u, 3 = 1.5u, 4 = 2u, 5 = 3u, never above your max bet.

    By Kelly. If you enter your own win probability, the stake is the Kelly fraction of your bankroll times your multiplier. If your probability is below the break-even implied by the odds, you get a "pass".

    Recommendations are rounded to the nearest 0.25u. They are a sizing tool, not a guarantee. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
